Will the emails actually sound like me, or will they sound like every other coach?

Voice matching is the entire point of our process. Before writing a single word, we spend time studying how you actually communicate — your podcast episodes, your existing emails, your social posts, your specific phrases. We then mirror this throughout every email we write. If anything sounds off, we rewrite it until it sounds exactly like you. This is our guarantee, not just a promise.

Do I have to commit to a monthly retainer, or is this a one-time project?

The Launch Sequence is a one-time project. You pay once, receive your complete 8-email sequence, and use it for every launch going forward — forever. There is zero obligation to continue working with us after delivery. Many clients come back for additional sequences or move to a monthly retainer after seeing their first launch results, but that is entirely your choice.

What if I have not launched recently or my list is small?

No recent launch is fine — we assess your list health, existing email setup, and welcome sequence to map what your next launch needs. As for list size: a smaller, highly engaged list with a strong sequence consistently outperforms a large, disengaged list with no structure. We have seen 2,000-subscriber lists outperform 50,000-subscriber lists. The sequence matters more than the size.

Can I use the sequence again for future launches?

100% — the sequence is yours permanently. You own every email, the strategy framework, and the segmentation logic we build. Most clients adapt and reuse the same sequence across 3–6 launches per year, updating specific details like dates, prices, and offer names. The structure, voice, and behavioural logic carries across every launch.

How is ByteRoc different from a freelance copywriter?

A freelance copywriter typically handles copy only — you still need to manage the strategy, design, platform setup, segmentation, and testing yourself. ByteRoc handles all of it: strategy, copy in your voice, design, platform configuration, segmentation setup, and testing. One project, one team, everything delivered. No disappearing mid-project — we have a structured delivery process with milestones and reviews built in.
