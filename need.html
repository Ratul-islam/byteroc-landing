FAQ

Frequently asked questions about our email marketing services

Everything you want to know before reaching out — answered honestly and in full.

How fast will I see results from your email marketing services?

Most clients see measurable improvements within 30–45 days. Open rates improve first as we optimise subject lines and sender reputation. Automation revenue — cart recovery, welcome flows, post-purchase sequences — typically activates within 30–60 days once flows are live and tested. Full strategic impact from complete segmentation and A/B testing usually shows clearly in month 3. We set these expectations transparently from day one.

What makes ByteRoc different from hiring a freelance email marketer?

A freelancer is typically skilled in one area — design, or copywriting, or strategy. Rarely all three. ByteRoc gives you a coordinated team of specialists who work together daily on your account. We're always available — no "out of office" surprises before your biggest campaign launch. You get consistent strategic thinking and cross-disciplinary execution, not disconnected project-by-project work from a single person.

Do I own all the email templates, flows, and strategies ByteRoc builds?

Yes — 100%. Every template, flow, copy document, strategy file, and campaign belongs to you permanently, from the moment we build it. We don't lock you into our systems or make it painful to leave. If you ever stop working with us, you take everything with you. We build assets for your business, not dependencies on ours. This is guaranteed in our engagement agreement from day one.

Which email platforms and ESPs does ByteRoc work with?

We specialise in Klaviyo — we're a certified Klaviyo Partner — and work with Mailchimp, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Omnisend, Drip, and others. If your current platform is limiting your growth or you need more powerful automation, we can migrate you to Klaviyo as part of onboarding. We've completed dozens of zero-data-loss migrations with a battle-tested process that typically takes under two weeks.

What exactly is included in the free email marketing audit?

A comprehensive review of: your current automation flows (which ones exist, which are missing), recent campaign performance compared against industry benchmarks, list health metrics and deliverability score, opt-in strategy analysis, and email template design quality. You receive a written report within 48 hours with specific, prioritised recommendations ranked by revenue impact. There's no sales pitch attached — just a clear, honest picture of where your email program is and where it should be.

How much of my time does working with ByteRoc require each month?

Very little — this is a genuinely done-for-you service. Your core involvement is: reviewing and approving campaign content before it sends (usually 10–15 minutes per batch), responding to Slack messages within 24 hours, and joining an optional 30-minute weekly check-in call. The strategy, copywriting, design, A/B testing, sending, and reporting is handled entirely by your ByteRoc team. Your job is to grow your business, not manage an agency.

How much does email marketing agency pricing typically cost?

Email marketing agency costs vary widely. Boutique agencies and entry-level retainers typically start at $800–$1,500/month. Mid-market full-service agencies run $2,000–$6,000/month. Top-tier specialists like Flowium or Hustler Marketing charge $5,000–$15,000+/month. ByteRoc offers three transparent tiers: Starter ($800/mo), Growth ($1,800/mo), and Scale ($4,000/mo) — all with full team access, transparent reporting, and no lock-in contracts. Start with a free audit to find the right fit.

Do you work with new businesses that don't have an email list yet?

Yes — and building right from scratch is often easier than fixing a broken foundation. We set up your entire email infrastructure, opt-in strategy, and welcome sequence from day one so you start building an engaged, revenue-generating list from your very first subscriber. We've helped several brands go from zero subscribers to a five-figure monthly email revenue channel within 90 days of starting.
