Pricing FAQ

Your pricing questions,
answered honestly

How much does email marketing agency pricing typically cost?

Email marketing agency costs range widely. Entry-level boutique agencies start at $500–$1,000/month. Mid-market full-service agencies run $1,500–$5,000/month. Top-tier specialists like Flowium and Hustler Marketing charge $5,000–$15,000+/month. ByteRoc sits at the premium mid-market level: $800–$4,000/month depending on scope, with transparent pricing you can see before any call.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. All ByteRoc retainer plans are month-to-month — no minimum term, no exit fees, no lock-in. We believe agencies that lock clients in contractually are compensating for results that don't speak for themselves. We earn your business every month through performance. If you want to leave, you give us 30 days' notice and take everything we've built with you.

What's included in the pricing — is copywriting extra?

Full email copywriting and design are included in every plan — no separate "creative fee." Many agencies charge for copy and design separately, which means that $1,500/month retainer can quickly become $3,000+/month when you add the real deliverables. With ByteRoc, what you see is what you pay.

Do I keep the emails, flows, and templates if I stop working with ByteRoc?

Yes — 100%, permanently. Every template, flow, strategy document, and campaign we build belongs to you from the moment we build it. Your Klaviyo account remains yours. Your list remains yours. Your flows remain yours. We don't hold anything hostage to retain clients.

How fast will I see a return on my investment?

Most Growth plan clients see their plan cost recovered within the first 30–45 days through cart recovery flows and improved campaign performance alone. The average ByteRoc client generates a 6–8x return on their retainer fee within 90 days. That said, we never guarantee specific revenue numbers — results depend on your list size, current performance, and industry. We'll give you realistic expectations at your free audit.

Can I start with a one-time project and upgrade to a retainer later?

Absolutely — and this is one of the most common paths. Many clients start with the Email Foundation Setup or a Launch Sequence to see our quality before committing to a retainer. If you decide to upgrade within 60 days of completing a one-time project, we credit 50% of the project fee toward your first month's retainer.

Do you offer discounts for annual commitment?

Yes — clients who commit to an annual plan save 15% on their monthly rate. Use the billing toggle above to see annual pricing. Annual plans are invoiced monthly (no upfront lump sum required) and include a quarterly strategy session as a bonus.

What if I need something not listed in the standard packages?

Use the custom quote form above. Whether you need a white-label arrangement, multi-brand management, an unusually high campaign volume, or a completely unique scope — describe what you need and we'll respond with a specific proposal within 24 hours. There's no obligation to proceed.
