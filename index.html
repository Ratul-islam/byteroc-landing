FAQ

Everything you want to know
before reaching out

Answered honestly — no fluff.

How fast will I see results?

Most clients see measurable improvements within 30–45 days — open rates lift first as we optimize subject lines and sender reputation. Automation revenue kicks in at 30–60 days once flows are live. Full strategic impact shows in month 3, when the full content calendar, segmentation, and A/B testing are all working together.

What makes ByteRoc different from a freelancer?

A freelancer is typically skilled in one area — design, or copy, or strategy. Rarely all three. ByteRoc gives you a coordinated team of specialists who work together every day on your account. We're always available — no "out of office" surprises before a big campaign launch. And you get consistent strategic thinking, not project-by-project work.

Do I own the templates, flows, and strategies you build?

100% yes. Every template, flow, copy document, strategy file, and campaign we build belongs to you permanently. We don't lock you into our systems. If you ever stop working with us, you take everything. We build assets for your business, not dependencies on ours.

What ESP platforms do you work with?

We specialize in Klaviyo and work with Mailchimp, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Omnisend, and others. If you're on a platform that's limiting your growth, we can migrate you as part of onboarding — we've run dozens of zero-data-loss migrations and have the process completely dialed in.

What's actually in the free audit?

A full review of your automation flows (are you missing key ones?), recent campaign performance vs. industry benchmarks, list health and deliverability score, opt-in strategy analysis, and template design quality. You receive a written report in 48 hours with specific, prioritized recommendations. No sales pitch attached — just a clear picture of where your email is and where it should be.

How much of my time does this require?

Very little — this is a done-for-you service. Your involvement is: reviewing and approving content before it sends, responding to our Slack messages within 24 hours, and an optional 30-minute weekly check-in call. Strategy, writing, design, testing, and sending is fully handled by us. You focus on growing your business.

What does a typical month look like?

Week 1: Content calendar approved, campaigns written and designed. Week 2: First campaigns sent, flow performance reviewed, A/B tests running. Week 3: Mid-month optimization pass, second campaign batch sent. Week 4: Monthly performance report delivered, next month's strategy proposed. You review and approve — we execute everything else.

Do you work with new businesses with no email list yet?

Yes — and it's actually easier to build right from scratch than to fix a broken foundation. We set up your entire email infrastructure, opt-in strategy, and welcome sequence from day one, so you start building an engaged list that drives revenue from your very first subscriber.
